About Me

Emmanuel KOLADE

I'm a print & digital designer based in Nigeria, specialising in bold designs that grab attention and drive action. I work with brands, event organisers, and businesses to create promotional and advertising graphics that get seen and get results.


My core design principles include: bold type, strong focal point, controlled composition, purposeful imagery and well planned hierarchy— every layout, image, colour, and typeface is chosen with care and purpose. Great design isn't just beautiful; it's precise, purposeful, and built to make your message impossible to ignore.
