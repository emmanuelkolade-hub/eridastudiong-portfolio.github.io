About the Designer

Founder & Creative Director

I'm a print & digital designer based in Nigeria, specialising in designs that grab attention and drive action. Through ERIDA STUDIO NG, I work with brands, event organisers, and businesses to create flyers, posters, and social media graphics that get seen and get results.


My approach blends strong visual hierarchy with meticulous craft — every layout, typeface, and colour is chosen with intention. Great design isn't just beautiful; it's precise, purposeful, and built to make your message impossible to ignore.
